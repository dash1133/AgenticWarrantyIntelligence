**BEAT 4**

Agentic Warranty Intelligence deploys a swarm of agents, each paired with the person who owns that part of the process. The claims analyst, the quality engineer, purchasing and the controller each oversee agents that do the routine drafting, collecting and presenting. Each agent does its own task, but they all operate from one coherent data record. The claim, the contract, the lot and the cost become one source of truth.

---

**TRANSITION**

Here is how it works.

---

**BEAT 5**

In the claims hub, every claim and debit note runs through six checks against the contract. When a check fails, the dispute agent drafts the challenge with the clause and the history attached. Your claims analyst reviews it and sends it.

---

**BEAT 6**

At the plant, the early warning agent runs around the clock, linking every claim to lot, shift and defect code. When a pattern forms, it clusters the failures, opens a process improvement work item and flags the lots at risk. Your quality engineer approves containment.

---

**BEAT 7**

In procurement, where a Tier 2 or Tier 3 part caused the failure, the recovery agent drafts the chargeback under that supplier's contract. The agent tracks recovery status through to settlement. Your purchasing lead approves the chargeback. Where the part is your own, the cost stays with you.

---

**BEAT 8**

In finance, the reconciliation agent matches every OEM debit note to the approved claim and flags the variance. Your controller approves the posting. The agent then reports net cost by product, plant, region and OEM. That is your true warranty profitability. The same agent runs what-if scenarios on that record, so you can test a reserve before you set it.

---

The agent swarm assists. Your people make the call.
